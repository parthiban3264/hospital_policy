Ramchin Hospital Management System

Last Updated • March 7, 2026

About the Application

Ramchin Hospital Management App helps healthcare institutions manage patient records, staff operations, pharmacy services, billing, laboratory reports, and hospital administration in a secure digital environment.

1
Information We Collect

Staff Information

  • Full name
  • Role (Admin, Doctor, Nurse, Cashier, Lab Technician)
  • Contact details
  • Login credentials
  • Profile information

Patient Information

  • Patient name
  • Age and gender
  • Contact information
  • Medical records and treatment history
  • Prescriptions and reports
  • Admission and discharge details
  • Billing information
2
How We Use Information
  • Managing patient records
  • Handling appointments and treatments
  • Processing billing and payments
  • Managing pharmacy inventory
  • Generating hospital reports
  • Managing staff roles and permissions
  • Improving system functionality and security
3
Role-Based Access

The application uses role-based access control to ensure that only authorized hospital staff can access specific data.

  • Manage Out-Patient consultations
  • Manage In-Patient treatment records
  • Write digital prescriptions
  • Order laboratory tests and scans
  • View test results and patient history
  • Monitor treatment progress
4
Ward Management
  • Assigning rooms and beds
  • Tracking patient location
  • Managing ward transfers
  • Monitoring treatment progress
  • Handling discharge process
5
Payment Processing
  • Registration fees
  • Consultation charges
  • Laboratory test payments
  • Pharmacy billing
  • In-patient room charges
6
Data Security
  • Secure login authentication
  • Role-based access control
  • Restricted patient data access
  • Secure database storage
  • Protection against unauthorized access
7
Data Sharing

Ramchin Hospital Management App does not sell or rent personal or medical information. Data may only be shared with authorized hospital staff or when required by law.

8
User Access
  • View and update profile information
  • Change account password
  • Access information based on assigned role
9
Children's Privacy

The application is intended for use by authorized hospital staff only. Any patient data involving minors is handled by healthcare professionals.

10
Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements, legal requirements, or system updates.
