Ramchin Hospital Management System

Privacy Policy

Welcome to the Ramchin Hospital Management System. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect sensitive personal and medical information of patients and authorized hospital staff.

This application is designed strictly for internal use by authorized healthcare professionals and hospital staff only.

1
Information We Collect

Patient Information

  • Full name, age, gender
  • Contact details (phone, address)
  • Medical history, diagnosis, and treatment records
  • Prescriptions and laboratory reports
  • Admission and discharge details
  • Billing and payment information

Staff Information

  • Name and role
  • Contact details
  • Login credentials (securely encrypted)
  • Profile and employment-related information

Clinical & Technical Data

  • Consultation notes and treatment history
  • Test results and patient care records
  • Device information and IP address
  • System logs and usage data
2
How We Use Information
  • Managing patient diagnosis and treatment
  • Maintaining electronic medical records (EMR)
  • Scheduling appointments and consultations
  • Handling lab, pharmacy, and clinical services
  • Processing billing and payments
  • Hospital reporting and administration
  • Security monitoring and system improvement
3
Role-Based Access Control
  • Only authorized staff can access data
  • Access limited based on job role
  • All activity is monitored and logged
4
Data Sharing
  • No selling or renting of data
  • Shared only with authorized staff
  • Shared with patients/guardians when required
  • Shared with trusted service providers
  • Shared when required by law
5
Data Security
  • HTTPS/TLS encrypted communication
  • Secure database storage
  • Authentication and access control
  • Activity monitoring
6
Data Retention
  • Maintained for medical continuity
  • Stored as per legal requirements
  • Deleted or archived when not needed
7
User Rights
  • Access personal data
  • Request correction
  • Request deletion (where allowed)
  • Request data copy
8
Children’s Privacy

Data of minors is handled with strict confidentiality under supervision of healthcare professionals and guardians.

9
Disclaimer

This system is for healthcare professionals only and does not provide medical advice to the public.

10
Policy Updates

This policy may be updated based on legal or system changes.
